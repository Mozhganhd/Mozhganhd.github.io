Selected work

A Vision-Language Model (VLM)-based Pipeline for End-to-End Procedural Modeling of Field-Grown Maize from Point Clouds

arXiv preprint, 2026

A vision-language model (Gemini) finds individual leaves in views of a 3D plant scan, and the pipeline turns them into an editable 3D model of the plant. On 100 field-grown maize plants it found 99.4% of hand-labeled leaves and replaced hours of manual tuning with about 14 minutes of processing per plant. I also prototyped an AI agent layer (LangChain/LangGraph) to run and coordinate the pipeline steps.

  • Paper

FloraForge: Procedural Generation of Editable and Analysis-Ready 3D Plant Geometric Models Using LLM-Assisted Template Design

Smart Agricultural Technology, 2026

Build editable 3D plant models by describing the plant to an LLM. The LLM writes and refines the Python modeling code step by step, and each plant is saved as a short, readable settings file that scientists can change without touching code. Tested on maize and mung bean, and used to simulate how much sunlight virtual fields capture.

MaizeField3D: A Curated 3D Point Cloud and Procedural Model Dataset of Field-Grown Maize from a Diversity Panel

A public dataset of 1,045 3D scans of field-grown maize from a diverse genetic panel. I led the labeling of 520 plants down to individual leaves and stalks, quality control, building a 3D model of every labeled plant, and the public release on Hugging Face and GitHub.

Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 2025

  2. Aug 2022 – present
    Graduate Research AssistantComputational Physics and Mechanics Laboratory, Iowa State University
    • Built an automated pipeline that uses a vision-language AI model (Gemini API) to find individual leaves in 3D plant scans and turn them into editable 3D plant models. Tested on 100 field-grown maize plants, it found 99.4% of hand-labeled leaves, matched the real scans more closely than our earlier method for 99 of the 100 plants, and replaced 2–6 hours of manual tuning per plant with about 14 minutes of processing.
    • Prototyped an AI agent layer (LangChain/LangGraph) on top of this pipeline, where agents ran each step, decided when to move to the next one, and ran independent steps in parallel.
    • Developed FloraForge, a method in which an LLM (GPT-5) writes and refines Python 3D-modeling code through step-by-step conversation, so plant scientists without 3D-modeling experience can create editable models of new plant species. Each plant is stored as a short, human-readable settings file, and the models were checked against real 3D scans.
    • Led the creation of MaizeField3D, a public dataset of 1,045 3D scans of field-grown maize from a diverse genetic panel: labeled 520 plants down to individual leaves and stalks, ran quality control, built a 3D model of every labeled plant, and packaged and released the dataset on Hugging Face and GitHub for other researchers.
    • Created an open-source method that converts raw laser scans of maize into smooth, editable 3D leaf models (NURBS surfaces), and used it to produce the 3D models for all 520 labeled MaizeField3D plants. Also reconstructed plants in 3D from photos and video using NeRF-based methods.
    • Ran sunlight-capture simulations on virtual maize and mung bean fields built from these 3D models to compare how much light the plants absorb across genotypes and growth stages.
    • Independently set up the hyperspectral imaging system and designed the hyperspectral and 3D laser scanning data collection for pepper plants, then trained machine learning models to detect nutrient deficiency and disease.
